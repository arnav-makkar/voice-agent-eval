Voice Agent Quality Flywheel.

A production-shaped system around the working Sarvam Indus v10 EMI agent: freeze it as the control, capture controlled voice calls, convert failures into tests, repair the correct layer, and promote a candidate only after matched voice validation.

01 / DECISION

Reset the evidence, keep the working agent.

The Sarvam integration and v10 prompt remain useful. The earlier synthetic corpus does not become the baseline, the evaluation set, or proof of improvement.

Keep

Working Indus runtime

Instant Outbound client, v10 agent, Shubh voice, Samsung TV account, Hindi/Hinglish prompt, synthetic customer variables and safe execution flow.

Reject as evidence

Old 150-scenario corpus

scenarios-v2.jsonl and fixture transcripts were created before observing representative failures. Archive them as exploratory work; exclude them from every metric and chart.

Why:

A simulator-generated baseline compared with a simulator-generated candidate can demonstrate internal consistency, but it cannot establish that the real voice agent improved. The new evidence chain starts with controlled calls through Indus.

02 / DEFINITION

What the system is—and is not.

This is an evaluation-driven, human-governed improvement system. “Self-improving” describes the recurring learning loop, not autonomous production deployment.

Build a system that converts real call failures into permanent tests, routes each failure to its actual owner, proposes bounded repairs, and releases a candidate only when independent evaluation shows better task completion with no safety regression.

Input

Call traces

Audio, transcript, agent version, scenario, Indus output variables, timestamps and reviewer labels.

Output

Release decision

A ranked failure backlog, candidate change, regression result and explicit promote, hold or roll-back decision.

Not claimed

Autonomous learning

No live self-editing, automatic deployment, production repayment lift, population fairness or legal certification.

Interview MVP objective
  • Improve a specific, working Hindi/Hinglish EMI agent.
  • Demonstrate the whole trace-to-release control loop.
  • Show one real failure becoming a regression test.
  • Show one candidate rejected by a safety or regression gate.
  • Show matched voice evidence for the frozen control versus candidate.
Production translation
  • Controlled calls become sampled production traces.
  • Human-labelled commitment becomes a real payment-ledger outcome join.
  • Manual labels become a calibrated audit sample.
  • Local scheduled runs become the weekly quality job.
  • Human promotion becomes staged traffic and rollback.

04 / MEASUREMENT CONTRACT

Measure the verbal objective honestly.

This experiment has no real lending app, customer account or payment ledger. It measures whether the agent obtains an explicit verbal commitment to log in and pay now—not whether a payment happens.

Primary interview metric

Confirmed Pay-Now Commitment Rate

An eligible call counts as a success only when the caller unambiguously agrees to log in and pay now, does not later contradict that commitment, and the agent commits no critical factual or safety violation.

Ground-truth authority

Human label, supported by evidence

The reviewer listens to the audio and inspects the transcript. Indus output variables are predictions scored against this gold label; the agent never grades itself.

Generate tests from observed failures.

The new synthetic dataset is built only after baseline annotation. Every synthetic row must point to a real seed trace, a confirmed failure cluster and a test purpose.

Next-reply tests

Repair the breaking turn

Freeze conversation state immediately before failure. Ask the candidate for only the next response. This is cheap and gives causal evidence.

Multi-turn tests

Verify eventual completion

Continue with an isolated caller simulator to test whether the repaired response converts into a valid final state.

Outcome tests

Verify extraction and safety

Check that explicit commitment is distinguished from acknowledgement, and that PTP, already-paid and dispute outcomes are classified without false completion claims.

Generation rules
  • Change one meaningful factor per controlled variant.
  • Keep account truth and safety policy immutable.
  • Never expose hidden caller state to the agent.
  • Deduplicate by intent and semantic similarity.
  • Human-review every regression and held-out case.
  • Mark all generated conversations as synthetic.

Judge calibration gate:

Before scoring the corpus, compare the LLM judge against all 12 human-labelled baseline calls plus 20 reviewed synthetic samples. Require at least 85% exact pass/fail agreement and zero missed P0 violations. Otherwise revise the rubric and rerun calibration.

First experiment selection

Choose one high-frequency owner

Do not change the prompt, workflow, tool and voice together. Select the highest-impact confirmed cluster that has one clear owner. This makes the before/after result interpretable.

Required negative result:

Keep the trace of at least one candidate that scores better on development data but is rejected for a P0, regression or held-out failure. This proves the gates control the optimizer.

10 / PROMOTION

Text is a gate. Voice is the proof.

The candidate must first survive offline evaluation. It then faces the same 12 scenario cards, callers and account state through Indus. Small-sample results are reported as matched counts, not as population-level statistical claims.

GATE 01

Development lift

Candidate improves the primary task score on failure-derived development tests. This chooses candidates; it is not final evidence.

Required · positive CPCR delta
GATE 02

Safety and regression

Zero P0 violations, correct disposition on every critical exclusion case, and no known critical failure returns.

Required · hard fail
GATE 03

Held-out transfer

Open the sealed 30 cases once. Require at least +10 percentage points on confirmed pay-now commitment, no guardrail decline and no slower median CTA.

Required · one attempt
GATE 04

Human prompt diff

Review exactly what changed. Reject fabricated claims, manipulative pressure, hidden policy changes or unnecessary verbosity even if aggregate score rises.

Required · signed review
GATE 05

Matched voice validation

Run 12 candidate calls against the unchanged cards. Report paired wins/losses, CPCR, turns to CTA, disposition accuracy, safety and voice failures versus the frozen control.

Final evidence
Promote

All hard gates pass

The candidate improves eligible matched calls, creates no P0 event, preserves exact account truth and does not make the first payment ask slower.

Hold or roll back

Any hard gate fails

Keep the control version, archive the candidate, document the failing trace and turn it into a permanent regression test for the next cycle.

12 / 7-DAY BUILD

Exact implementation order.

Build the evidence path before optimizing the prompt. Each day ends with a binary artifact gate.

DAY 1
Freeze + trace

Control version and local manifest

Export the working v10 agent and freeze it as CTRL. Define the call manifest and annotation schemas. Generate a local run_id before one consenting smoke-test call, store the returned Sarvam attempt_id, then verify the transcript and outputs join correctly.

Gate · frozen CTRL version; one run_id joins scenario, attempt and artifacts
DAY 2
Collect

12 human-played control calls

Use one consenting caller and the 12 frozen behavior cards, import audio/transcripts/outputs and record call cost. Replace only technically unusable calls; keep behavioral failures.

Gate · 12 complete immutable traces
DAY 3
Label

Gold set and evaluator calibration

Blind-label outcomes and breaking turns. Implement deterministic state checks and structured semantic judge. Audit all baseline calls. Iterate the judge until it meets the declared agreement and P0-recall thresholds.

Gate · calibrated scores reproduce human gold
DAY 4
Expand

Failure-derived test dataset

Confirm six clusters. Generate 20 development, five regression and five held-out variants per cluster. Human-review the 60 non-development cases, deduplicate and freeze split hashes in MLflow.

Gate · 120/30/30 with lineage and sealed held-out
DAY 5
Improve

Route failures and create candidates

Rank clusters. Choose one experiment owner. Run GEPA only if prompt-owned; otherwise implement one bounded tool, workflow or voice change. Cap at three candidates and preserve all run artifacts.

Gate · one finalist; one visible rejected candidate
DAY 6
Gate

Regression, held-out and human review

Run critical cases three times, apply hard safety and outcome-consistency gates, open held-out once, inspect the prompt/config diff and commit the finalist as the next Indus candidate version only if every rule passes.

Gate · signed promote-to-voice decision
DAY 7
Prove

12 matched candidate calls and CXO package

Repeat the unchanged caller cards. Blind-score calls. Produce paired results, failure examples, voice-transfer gap, cost ledger, limitations, weekly architecture and a two-minute demo narrative.

Gate · promote, hold or rollback from evidence
