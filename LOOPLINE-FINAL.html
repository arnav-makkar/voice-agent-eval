Objective assessment

What is excellent—and what is not.

Strong
System design

Evaluation, improvement and release are separate. The optimiser cannot grade or approve itself.

Execution truth

Scenarios assert disposition, backend state, required actions and guardrails. Sarvam-originated authenticated tool calls produced real isolated state mutation.

Experiment discipline

Manual and GEPA arms competed; a 28/30 optimiser result was rejected for severe per-case regressions. Failed candidates remain visible.

Auditability

Prompt, evaluator and dataset hashes; append-only tool events; audio; transcripts; state diffs; MLflow lineage; a signed HOLD decision.

Presentation product

Light and dark Loopline UI, seven deep episode demos, three replayable live pilots, exact metric contracts and a 30/60/90 production plan.

Weak / incomplete
Live voice quality

The repair round is not a matched improvement result. Two of three trials were invalid and the sole valid call still failed experience.

Punjabi control

The simulator declared English while speaking Punjabi, and the agent still failed to execute the required promise write.

Workflow enforcement

Prompt text alone did not eliminate repeated openings or guarantee tool-before-claim. Those belong in explicit turn/workflow state.

Infrastructure

The authenticated tunnel proves reachability, not production uptime. A managed endpoint, allowlisting, observability, retry policy and SLO are still required.

Business outcome

The measured proxy is an explicit payment commitment. There is no ledger join proving settled cash or collections lift.

The product

Three responsibilities. One closed loop.

01

Evaluation engine

  • Versioned hidden-goal scenarios
  • Realtime or stateful execution
  • Validity before scoring
  • Deterministic truth before judges
  • First break and owning component
02

Improvement engine

  • Routes prompt, extractor, tool, workflow, runtime or policy failures
  • Manual and optimiser arms
  • Versioned hypotheses and diffs
  • L0–L3 implemented; L4 argued; L5 deliberately refused
  • Negative candidates retained
03

Release controller

  • Exact paired per-case comparison
  • No severe regression can be averaged away
  • Fresh final opened once
  • Pilot before paid matched suite
  • Promote, hold or rollback—with a human
