02The objective take on the improvement work

What cannot be presented, and why

Two artifacts exist that beat the baseline — v4 (hand-written from the diagnosis: 120/180, blind 40/60) and v5 (GEPA seeded from v4, plus one human edit to its closing section: 129/180, blind 44/60). Both are real measurements, and both are contaminated as self-improvement claims: human-authored candidate text sits in their lineage. They stay in the repo as internal ablations. The presented ladder must be machine-only.

What is legitimately built and clean — the framework itself is allowed to be human-designed; that is engineering, not cheating. The line is drawn at candidate text:

  • The environment. Frozen 180-scenario benchmark pinned to the deployed account; the text-chat REST channel (a real conversation with the deployed agent every 7.8s); the authoring API (PUT …/apps/{id} — a candidate applied to the live draft in ~1s); journal-truth grading; sequential isolation with ledger reset.
  • The optimiser harness. GEPA Optimize Anything with rich per-scenario feedback, explicit objective, Pareto selection, dev-only train/val, blind 60 touched once. Gemini 2.5 Pro reflecting (Gemini 3 Pro is not enabled on this key).
  • The gate. Blind-split decision, guardrail-family veto, per-case regression listing.
  • The monitors. Invariants at precision 1.00; the failure taxonomy generator.

What is weak, stated plainly:

  • GEPA ran once, seeded from the contaminated v4, stopped early, with a 15-scenario valset — small enough that the champion won on selection noise and regressed on cases its parent handled. A human patch fixed that; the system should have.
  • Quality is unoptimised. The loop maximises journal correctness only. EVA-A/EVA-X, turn length, progression — measured at baseline, not in the reward.
  • No significance testing yet: at n=60 blind, +4 is suggestive, not proof. Paired tests on the full 180 are the honest instrument.
  • No voice re-verification. Chat-optimised wording must re-prove itself on the voice tier before any claim about the voice agent.

03Your questions, answered straight

Is GEPA run once, or stacked? What is the best way to use it?

Once so far, and that is the weakness. The right shape is generations, each with two phases:

  1. Explore — GEPA Pareto search from the current champion, budget ~300 live conversations, rich feedback per rollout.
  2. Consolidate — a second, short GEPA run whose trainset is the champion's own regressions versus its parent, with acceptance requiring no net loss. This is the step I performed by hand on v5 — a precedence ladder for the closing code — and it is precisely automatable: the evidence that taught me was sitting in the eval output. Making consolidation systemic is what removes the human from the loop.

Then the champion seeds the next generation, and — this is the part that makes it self-improving rather than once-improved — the trainset is refreshed from the new champion's residual failures. A single long run plateaus when its evidence is exhausted (ours flatlined from iteration 5 to 15). Stacked generations with fresh mined evidence compound.

What should be used apart from GEPA? Is it even enough?

No — GEPA is one stage of five. It is the search engine, and a search engine is not a self-improvement system. The other four stages are what make the claim serious (§04): label-free sensing, failure distillation into a growing replay suite, a statistical gate with guardrail veto, and versioned deploy with rollback. Two additions matter beyond the loop itself:

  • Beyond-prompt actuators. Several baseline defects were configuration, not prose — a tool description that mis-routed disputes, an unregistered tool. The authoring API exposes tool descriptions, the greeting, and state fields as text; GEPA optimises multi-component candidates natively. The prompt is just the first component.
  • Calibrated LLM judges. Invariants catch 28% of failures at precision 1.00; the rest need a judge reading transcript-versus-journal. The benchmark's labeled data is what makes judges honest: measure judge precision/recall against owner truth before trusting them on live traffic. A judge you haven't calibrated is a vibe.
What is the target?

Three, in order of what they prove:

  1. The clean number. A champion with a pure machine lineage — seeded from committed v3, zero human candidate text — beating base on the blind 60 with guardrails non-regressing and a paired significance test on the 180 (McNemar; the ablation's 29-fixed / 6-broken profile is p ≈ 0.0001, so the bar is reachable). Base is 34/60 blind; the contaminated ablation reached 44/60, which is the honest reference for what the clean run should approach.
  2. The compounding demonstration. Two generations, where generation 2 improves on generation 1 using evidence generation 1 could not have had. One optimisation is a result; two stacked is a system.
  3. The transfer check. The champion re-measured on voice — same 15 cards, same 5 bot-to-bot — because the deliverable is a voice agent, and chat is the cheap searchable proxy of it, not the product.
Are EVA-A and EVA-X being improved too?

Not yet — and saying otherwise would be false. The current reward is journal correctness. The plan adds quality as secondary Pareto objectives, which GEPA supports natively: turn length ≤25 words (computable directly), progression (no repeated pitch — computable), and judge-scored conciseness calibrated against the chat pilot's EVA numbers. Pareto matters here: quality as a second axis rather than a weighted sum means the loop cannot buy task completion with verbose robotic turns. Voice EVA-A/EVA-X get re-measured in the S7 transfer check, where the harness caveats (turn_taking is the synthetic caller's latency) are already documented.

04The framework — five stages, one growing memory

Designed to run on millions of conversations. The trick is that only sensing scales with traffic; everything expensive runs on distilled evidence.

StageWhat it doesStatusAt millions of calls
SenseJournal invariants on every conversation; calibrated LLM judges on a sample; outcome KPIs (promise-kept rate, escalation validity)invariants built, P=1.00 · judges designedinvariants free on 100% · judges on 1–5% sampled

SearchGEPA explore + consolidate over prompt, tool descriptions, greeting — applied via authoring API, evaluated by real conversations, scored from the journalexplore built · consolidate to buildO(hundreds) of rollouts per generation, independent of traffic volume
GateBlind split evaluated once; guardrail-family veto; per-case regression list; McNemar + Wilson intervalsgate built · stats to addsame suite, frozen and hashed
DeployDraft = staging, commit = production — Indus's own version model. Canary %-traffic, invariant-rate comparison, auto-rollback = re-apply parentapply/rollback proven over the API · commit stays owner-gatedcanary 1% → 100%; rollback in seconds

Why this scales: sensing is deterministic and free at any volume; judges are sampled; search cost depends on the distilled evidence, not the traffic. A system serving a million calls a day and one serving ten thousand run the same three-hundred-conversation generation — the million-call system just sees more failure modes per night, which makes each generation better informed, not more expensive.

The honest risk, named now

The clean run may land below the contaminated 129/180 — it starts from a weaker seed and must rediscover everything. That is acceptable, and the framing survives it: the claim is the system — sense, distill, search, gate, deploy, compounding across generations — and whatever the clean number is, it is real, significant or honestly labelled otherwise, and reproducible with one command. A slightly smaller true number beats a larger one you cannot defend.

The MIPROv2 graft — candidates now have two components

The GEPA candidate is no longer one text. It is {instructions, exemplars}: the exemplars component seeds from worked examples machine-mined from the baseline's own passing calls — shortest passing conversation per behaviour, journal writes shown at their position, selected by rule, authored by no one. GEPA's round-robin component selector then evolves the two on their own schedules, which is MIPRO's actual lesson: instructions tell, examples show, and they improve differently. Merge is enabled, so frontier specialists (the candidate that fixed disputes, the one that fixed escalations) can recombine instead of one being discarded. The trainset sampler is now severity-weighted: safety and fraud families get up to 4 failing slots, bookkeeping families 2 — search attention follows what a failure costs, not how often it occurs.

Consolidation is now the system's job

The step that contaminated v5 — a human repairing the champion's regressions — is Phase 4/6c: measure the champion on the full development split, hand its regressions (plus a protected sample of its own fixes) to a short second GEPA run seeded from the champion, and pick between the two by their score on the same 30-scenario valset. The evidence that taught the human is now fed to the machine.
