4What the morning delivers, and the decisions that are yours

The run makes no irreversible move: it never commits a version, never touches the serving v3, never spends a credit. You wake up to:

  • The ladder — v3 → gen-1 → gen-2, overall and blind, each rung with McNemar p, Wilson CI, the within-noise verdict, guardrail delta, words-per-turn.
  • The residual evidence — per-family failures of the final champion, the stuck bucket (your ranked targets if you take the manual rung later), and every candidate GEPA explored with scores.

Your calls, after reading it — none of these run tonight

  • Approve & commit the champion as v-next (rollback target v3).
  • S7 voice transfer: your 15 phone cards + 5 bot-to-bot on the champion (~5 credits/call).
  • Optional manual rung, separately labelled, guided by the stuck bucket.
  • Optional paraphrase robustness check: blind-30 with reworded caller lines, ~30 conversations.

5Commitments

  • No human-authored or human-edited candidate text anywhere in the lineage. Seed = committed v3; exemplars = machine-mined; every mutation model-proposed.
  • The blind 60 is used by no search phase; the fresh blind-30 is touched exactly once, by the final champion and by v3 for its denominator.
  • Any delta below 2σ is reported as within noise — gains and losses alike. Guardrail regression is a veto, not a trade.
  • Nothing beyond this file runs tonight. If the run crashes it resumes; it does not improvise.
