Listen

All forty calls, before and after

Two very different kinds of evidence, kept apart. Phone calls are a person on a real line. Bot-to-bot is a synthetic caller speaking to the live agent over audio, which is what makes voice testing repeatable. Every call ends with its full metric panel.

Start with a contrast. Pick a call, listen, then use the button under the player to hear the other version of the same situation.

Phone calls

15 situations, each run on the old agent and the new one, real handset

Bot-to-bot voice lab

5 situations, synthetic caller, live audio, judged metrics

These conversations are scored on the full metric set: three accuracy checks, two experience checks, and the execution check read from the permanent log.
