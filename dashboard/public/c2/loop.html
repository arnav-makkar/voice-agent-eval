Section 4 · The improvement loop

The search that wrote the fix — with no human writing a word

A prompt-search algorithm on its own is not a self-improving system. It needs something to tell it what is broken, something to decide whether a change is real, and something to ship or revert. Five stages close that loop around the deployed agent, and every candidate word of the champion prompt was produced by the search — none by hand.

The loop

Inside the search

The search stage is GEPA — reflective prompt evolution — run against the deployed agent itself, not a simulation. Each iteration: propose a candidate prompt, run it on a minibatch of live conversations, read the full evaluation traces, and let a reflector model reason about why failures happened before proposing the next mutation. Selection is Pareto — a candidate survives by being best at something, not average at everything — with merge crossing the best branches.

Two-component candidates

Generation 2 grafts the one idea worth stealing from MIPROv2: a candidate is not just instructions but {instructions, exemplars}, evolved separately. Exemplars are mined from the champion's own best transcripts — real calls where the write pattern was perfect — and the search learns which demonstrations teach the tool-calling model most.

The ablation isolates each component's worth on the blind split: baseline 33/60, instructions alone 49/60, both components 59/60. The exemplar component carries 38% of the total gain — the graft earned its place.

What the loop is not

Boundaries, stated plainly

  • No human-authored candidate text. The champion prompt is the search's output verbatim. The one human change — naming the platform's end_interaction function in the closing rule — came after the campaign, is declared on every results surface, and touches call termination, which was never a scored objective.
  • Persuasion was not an objective. The loop optimised execution truth: right writes, right arguments, exactly once. Objection handling and collection rate are the named next objective, with turns-to-capitulation as the candidate metric.
  • Two rungs, not twenty. Gen 1 → Gen 2 added +5 overall at p = 0.18 — positive, regression-free, and honestly below significance. Compounding is a hypothesis the next campaign tests, not a claim this one makes.
