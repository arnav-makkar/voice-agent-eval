The claim, in one paragraph

A collections voice agent's job is not to sound competent — it is to leave the correct trace in the system of record. This project builds the instrument that measures exactly that, uses it to diagnose a deployed agent's failures, lets an automated search fix them with no human writing a single candidate word, and then proves the fix on three progressively harsher channels: headless text, a real phone line, and full-duplex speech-to-speech. Every number on this site is reproducible from artifacts in the repository, and every claim about a tool is read from an append-only journal the agent cannot talk its way into.
