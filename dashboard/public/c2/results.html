Two things moved, seven held, nothing regressed. The thresholds here are forgiving, so a call can improve noticeably inside a cell that still reads as an unchanged tick. The distress call is the clearest example: it stopped repeating itself and cut five records down to the two that were needed, all inside one tick.

Why turn taking is not on this table

The turn taking metric needs per turn audio boundaries from the caller's side. In this setup those come back empty, so it returns zero for every call on both versions. A zero that means "no data" is not a finding, and averaging it in would drag a score down for a reason that has nothing to do with the agent. It is excluded rather than quietly included.

Call 5 fails on both versions, in exactly the same way.

The agent checks the account, holds the figure, says "I will note your response", and writes nothing. It is the last remaining case of the exact failure this whole project was built to catch, and it is the first target of the next round.

It stays on this page on purpose. A scoreboard with a visible hole is easier to believe than a perfect one.
