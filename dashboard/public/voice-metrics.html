This is the honest shape of the result. Most of EVA's thresholds are lenient — faithfulness and conciseness pass at ≥ 0.5 — so a call can improve substantially without changing its verdict. The distress scenario is the clearest case: conciseness rose 0.667 → 1.0 and progression 0.5 → 1.0, both real improvements a listener can hear, and both sitting inside a single ✓ in the grid above.

That is exactly why the last column exists. Exactly-once writes is Loopline's own check, read from the tool journal rather than the transcript, and it is the only column that separates the two versions across more than one scenario.

The four families

Task completion

deterministic== 1.0

Hashes the scenario database after the call and compares it to the expected end state. No judge, no transcript — it asks whether the world changed the way the scenario required.

Faithfulness

LLM judge≥ 0.5

Whether the agent's claims match what actually happened and what it was given. Catches an agent that invents a balance, a date, or a policy it was never told.

Agent speech fidelity

LLM judge≥ 0.95

Entity-level check that names, amounts and dates survived the agent's speech intact. The strictest threshold in the set, because a mangled amount is a failed call.

Progression

LLM judge≥ 0.5

Did the conversation move forward, or circle? This is the metric that caught v3 repeating its distress response almost verbatim.

Conciseness

LLM judge≥ 0.5

Whether the agent said what it needed in the space it needed. Not word count — a judge reading the whole conversation.

Turn taking

audio timestamps≥ 0.8

Interruptions, overlap and latency, scored from per-turn audio segment boundaries. Not measurable here — see below.

Valid end · caller plausibility

LLM judge≥ 0.5

Validation metrics. They grade the test, not the agent: did the conversation end properly, and did the simulated caller behave like a real person? A failure here invalidates the call as evidence rather than counting against the agent.

Exactly-once writes

journalno duplicates

Loopline's own check. Every required tool write appears in the append-only journal exactly once. Reading the ledger rather than the transcript means a spoken claim can never satisfy it.

Two things this page will not claim

Turn taking is not reported

It scores 0.0 on all ten calls, both versions. The metric needs per-turn audio segment boundaries from the caller's side; in this harness those lists come back empty, so it returns zero for every turn rather than measuring anything. A zero that means "no data" is not a finding, and averaging it into EVA-X would drag a composite down for a reason that has nothing to do with the agent. It is shown as – above and excluded from every total.

Five scenarios is directional, not significant

Three of the five already passed every agent-side check on v3, so there was no room to improve them. Two moved, none regressed. That is a real signal about the duplicate-write defect and about pay-now, and it is not a statistical claim — the 180-scenario chat tier is where the statistics live.
